Privacy policy

Updated: September 2026

Ripper Search is a Chrome extension that helps you search the Ripper.Store forum.

Summary

  • We do not collect your data on our servers.
  • Everything stays in your browser, except when you use the forum or stores normally.

What is stored on your computer

Your settings, search history, monitored requests, and download list are stored locally in Chrome.

Ripper.Store forum

If you are logged in, the extension uses your session to search, upvote, and post on the forum. We never see your password.

Stores (Booth, Gumroad, etc.)

The extension reads the name and link of the current page to build the forum search. None of this goes to Ripper Search servers.

Chrome permissions

PermissionWhy
StorageSave your preferences and history
CookiesUse your forum login
NotificationsAlert when a new download appears
DownloadsDownload files when you click

What we do not do

  • No tracking or ads
  • No selling of data
  • No sign-up in the extension
