# User guide — Ripper Search

[← Languages](README.md) · [Install](../readme/en.md)

## Before you start

1. [Install the extension](../readme/en.md#download-and-install)
2. Log in at [forum.ripper.store](https://forum.ripper.store/login)
3. Open a product on Booth (or another supported store)
4. Click the **Ripper Search** icon in Chrome

If you see "Log in to the forum", click **Open login**.

---

## Search tab

- **Search by name** or **by URL**
- **Any word** or **All words**
- **Downloads found** vs **Discussions**
- Buttons: **Download**, **Bump**, **★ Favorite**, **♡ Upvote**, **Post LF request**

---

## Requests tab

Tracks favorites and LF requests. Status: moderation queue, under review, monitoring, new DL, resolved.

**Refresh scan** checks for updates. Notifications in **Settings**.

---

## Downloads tab

Paste a link or use **Download** on results with DL.

---

## History tab

Saved searches. **Repeat** to search again.

---

## Settings tab

Language, LF tags, bump, export/import, enabled stores.

---

## FAQ

### Do I need to be logged in?

Yes.

### LF request didn't show up?

It goes through **moderation** first. Check the **Requests** tab.

### Download didn't work?

Protected links open in a new tab. Some sites require human verification.

### Extension not verified?

Normal when installing from ZIP. Download only from [official Releases](https://github.com/T1NG4/ripper-search/releases).

### How to update?

Download the new version, remove the old one at `chrome://extensions`, and install again.
